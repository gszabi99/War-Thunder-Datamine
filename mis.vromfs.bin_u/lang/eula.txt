5. HOW LONG WE PROCESS YOUR DATA

We process your data for as long as necessary for the processing purposes described in this Policy, and then we delete or anonymize it. Some data may be kept longer where the law requires it or where we need it to resolve disputes or safeguard our other interests.

In general, we retain data associated with your Gaijin Account for as long as your account remains active, so that we can provide the Services and maintain your progress and purchases. The precise periods for data storage vary depending on the category of personal data and purpose of processing. 

Factors we consider in determining these periods include the minimum retention period prescribed by law, the period during which a claim can be made with respect to a contract or other matter, whether the purpose of data collection was achieved, and whether data can be anonymized. For example, gameplay recordings are stored for 14 or 30 calendar days depending on the Game, and support tickets are retained for 5 years or until your account is deleted, whichever comes first. You may delete your account in the Personal Area of your profile or request deletion of your Gaijin Account by contacting Gaijin Support (See Section 9: Your Rights).

Please note that we may retain limited user data even after your deletion request, where:

• We are required by law to store data (for example, purchase records are retained under tax regulations);
• We need it to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims (for example, records are relevant to an ongoing dispute or to enforcing our Terms and Conditions);
• Data is needed for security, cheating and fraud prevention (for example, to prevent ban evasion and repeated abuse);
